About the Program

I'm so grateful to have been able to experience Ohio State's first Around the World with Disney Imagineering program. It was such a unique opportunity to explore the intersection of engineering, storytelling, and design within Disney Imagineering. Through experiences in both California and Japan, I got a behind the scenes look into Disney's creative process. The program challenged me to adapt to new environments, understand global perspectives, and collaborate with other OSU engineering students on real-world design projects.

Pitch an Attraction

As part of the program, I worked in a multidisciplinary team to develop and pitch an original Disney attraction concept. Our challenge was to create an experience centered around the theme of nature and technology, and we chose to base our attraction on Pixar's upcoming film Hoppers. We transformed an initial boat ride concept into a fully developed guest experience by designing the storyline, creating concept art, and producing a final pitch video filmed throughout the California Disney parks. My role focused on translating our initial ideas into visual designs to clearly communicate our team's vision. This project strengthened my collaboration, leadership, and creative problem-solving skills while also reinforcing my passion for combining technology with creative storytelling.

Problem Portfolio

To better understand how my Computer Science & Engineering background fits within Disney Imagineering, I developed a problem portfolio focused on interactive queue experiences. Prior to the trip, I researched the growing reliance on mobile devices can negatively impact guest engagement. The project explored ways to balance technology and storytelling by proposing interactive queue experiences that enhance immersion. Through research and analysis, I examined how technology can be used thoughtfully to improve guest experiences while preserving the storytelling that makes Disney attractions unique. Meeting Disney Imagineers

The most impactful experiences in the program was getting the opportunity to meet with Disney Imagineers in both California and Tokyo.

California

The highlight of the program was visiting Walt Disney Animation Studios in Burbank, California, and meeting with Disney professionals. We had the opportunity to tour the studio, learn about Disney internship programs, and speak with an Ohio State alum currently working in the animation industry. She shared how she combines computer science and art through her work as a Technical Director and discussed the variety of career paths available within animation. Learning about her journey helped me discover my own interest in technical direction, a role that bridges creative teams and software developers. This experience gave me a clearer vision of my future career goals and reinforced my desire to combine technical problem-solving with creative storytelling.

🖼

Tokyo, Japan

During our visit to Tokyo DisneySea, we had the opportunity to participate in a guided tour of the park and attend a panel discussion with Disney Imagineers. The Imagineers shared the design philosophy behind one of the world's most immersive theme parks and the amount of work involved in creating these environments. Throughout the tour, our guide highlighted storytelling details intentionally built into every space. He explained to us the details in the geology and architecture that most guests never notice. Hearing directly from the Imagineers and seeing these details firsthand deepened my appreciation for themed entertainment design. It was truly incredible to see the collaboration and creativity required to create these immersive guest experiences.

Career Development

This program played a major role in shaping my future career goals. Through interactions with Disney Imagineers, exposure to animation and themed entertainment, and collaborative design projects, I learned more about careers that combine technology with creativity. This experience reinforced my goal of pursuing opportunities where I can apply my computer science background to visual storytelling, design, and user experience. Beyond technical growth, the program strengthened my confidence and adaptability while encouraging me to continue stepping outside my comfort zone to pursue new opportunities.
